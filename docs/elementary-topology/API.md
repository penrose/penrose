The machine-readable [domain vocabulary audit](./domain-vocabulary-audit.json)
records all 59 available sections (58 chapter sections plus the appendix), the

Missing source pages remain explicit gaps; this vocabulary does not reconstruct

## Modules and composition

- [set-theory.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/set-theory.ts) exports the shared basic factory, full elementary set-theory factory and reusable algebra factory. The standalone `setTheory` domain preserves the existing figure interfaces while adding functions, orders, cardinality, equivalence, groups and background rings/ideals.
- [decimal-expansions.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/decimal-expansions.ts) composes positive integers, decimal expansions and separate known finite prefixes. `complementedDiagonalPrefix` checks the visible 0/1 diagonal positions without assuming values for unseen digits or proving uncountability from a sample.
- [topological-vocabulary.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/topological-vocabulary.ts) composes abstract later-chapter concepts with an existing base; `declareTopologicalVocabularyWithGroups` reuses already-declared algebra. [point-set-topology.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/point-set-topology.ts) performs the standard composition and retains its existing geometry-specific subclasses.
- [finite-set-theory.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/finite-set-theory.ts) validates concrete functions, partial orders, equivalence classes, group laws, normal subgroups and quotients. [finite-topology.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/finite-topology.ts) checks actual finite topology, filter and directed-net models.
- [metric-spaces.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/metric-spaces.ts), [metric-completeness.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/metric-completeness.ts) and [metric-covers.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/metric-covers.ts) expose geometric metric calculations and analytically justified sequence/cover witnesses.
- [plane-curves.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/plane-curves.ts) validates immutable joined cubic/circular loops and supplies conservative whole-curve disk bounds. [loop-operations.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/loop-operations.ts) evaluates loop reparameterizations and finite Fourier loops. [surface-topology.ts](../../packages/bloom/src/domains/surface-topology.ts) exposes sphere charts, torus factor loops, based conjugation and graph-count arithmetic.

All declarations in a composed domain belong to the same builder; seal the domain
only after composing its factories. Predicates have no automatic inference.
Author-supplied assertions about infinite spaces require mathematical
justification. Finite samples do not prove infinite convergence, compactness or
homotopy identities. Constructors type-check metadata but are not theorem
provers; use validated helper data when available. Higher homotopy, homology and
cohomology are named background topics in the source conclusion, represented by
identifying declarations without invented definitions or a computation engine.

The reusable [group kernel Style](../../packages/bloom/src/styles/group-kernels.tsx)
illustrates §1.4 Exercise 2 with two different whole finite models. The native
[operation table Style](../../packages/bloom/src/styles/group-tables.tsx) validates
all finite group laws from recorded products; the
[decimal table Style](../../packages/bloom/src/styles/decimal-tables.tsx) renders
known prefixes with native equation metrics. These examples keep all mathematical
values in Substance and visual arrangement in Style.

Use the public `@penrose/bloom/domains/*`, `@penrose/bloom/styles/*` and
`@penrose/bloom/examples/*` subpaths for these modules. Book build factories
accept final `FigureRenderOptions` and forward interaction to their explicit
Style policies. A direct `diagram({ interactive: ... })` enables the built-in
annotation policy; whole constructions require the Style's option as well.
Zero jitter retains canonical placement, and a different variation seed with
nonzero jitter samples the enabled visual layout.

Ordinary figures move labels with their knockouts; Figure 4.5 moves the disk as
one construction. The cover translates its boundary and whole curve family;
the two source tables translate all cells and grid lines together. Figure 11.24
moves eleven complete spaces, retaining holes, cycles and joins. Kernel originals
move nodes with their incident map arrows. Interaction preserves immutable
mathematics and changes neither model parameters nor homotopy time.

Finite helpers validate actual finite models. The metric-cover witness checks
strict overlap and both endpoints of a compact real interval. The curve-disk
certificate checks whole pieces through outward-rounded bounds; a failed
certificate may be unresolved at its subdivision budget. Sphere loop contraction
requires an omitted-pole chart and does not contract the sphere itself. Graph
cycle-rank arithmetic uses supplied incidence/component counts. These premises
are separate from rendered samples.
