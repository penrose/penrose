# 1 Preliminaries

This chapter transcribes the available pages of the supplied second-edition scan of Michael C. Gemignani's _Elementary Topology_. Printed pages 4 and 13 are missing from that scan. The wording, exercises, references, mathematical notation, and example numbering are preserved. Line breaks and line-end hyphenation are adapted for reading on the web. Running headers and page-number footers are recorded in the page map rather than repeated in the text. Notes in these boxes are editorial; the exercise instructions in the transcribed text belong to the book.
